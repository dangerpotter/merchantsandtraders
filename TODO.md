## Planned Features by Version

### v0.2.0 - Enhanced Trade Goods & Regions (Q1 2026)

#### Trade Goods Improvements
- [ ] **Regional specialization system**
  - Counties dynamically develop trade goods based on terrain, culture, development
  - Historical trade good locations (Silk Road, Spice Islands, etc.)
  - Discovery events for rare goods in unexplored regions

- [ ] **Seasonal trade good variation**
  - Winter reduces grain production
  - Summer increases wine demand
  - Seasonal pricing modifiers
  - Event chains based on seasons

- [ ] **Trade good quality tiers**
  - Common, Fine, Exceptional quality levels
  - Quality affects price multipliers
  - Quality events (good harvest, poor silk batch, etc.)
  - Reputation tied to quality sold

#### Regional Markets
- [ ] **Major trade hubs**
  - Constantinople, Venice, Baghdad, Cairo as special markets
  - Hub-specific events and opportunities
  - Price differentials between hubs
  - Hub control affects global prices

- [ ] **Regional price variation**
  - Silk cheap in China, expensive in Europe
  - Wine cheap in France, expensive in Middle East
  - Arbitrage opportunities for smart merchants
  - Dynamic pricing based on distance from source

## Known Issues to Fix

### Critical (Must Fix for v0.2.0)

- [ ] **Bankruptcy loop** - Players can get stuck in bankruptcy state
- [ ] **Route health overflow** - Health can exceed 100 in some cases
- [ ] **AI creates unprofitable routes** - AI doesn't validate profitability
- [ ] **Market price crash** - Prices can drop to 0 in extreme cases
- [ ] **Variable persistence** - Some variables don't save correctly

### High Priority

- [ ] **Event frequency balancing** - Some events fire too often/rarely
- [ ] **Localization typos** - Grammar and spelling issues
- [ ] **Decision costs** - Some decisions too cheap/expensive
- [ ] **Route profit calculation** - Edge cases produce wrong values
- [ ] **GUI scaling** - Doesn't work on all resolutions

### Medium Priority

- [ ] **Performance with 100+ routes** - Lag during monthly pulse
- [ ] **Save file bloat** - Large save files with many merchants
- [ ] **AI merchant death** - AI routes don't clean up properly
- [ ] **War disruption timing** - Routes damaged before war actually starts
- [ ] **Tooltip accuracy** - Some tooltips show wrong values

### Low Priority (Polish)

- [ ] **Text formatting** - Inconsistent capitalization
- [ ] **Icon mismatches** - Some decisions have wrong icons
- [ ] **Event variety** - More flavor event variants needed
- [ ] **Decision descriptions** - Some are too terse
- [ ] **GUI aesthetics** - Windows could be prettier

## Long-term Vision

### Merchant Sim Elements

Eventually, Merchants and Traders should feel like a complete merchant simulation game within CK3, comparable to:

- **Port Royale** - Caribbean trade simulation
- **Patrician** - Hanseatic League merchant sim
- **The Guild** - Medieval merchant/crafting sim
- **Anno series** - City building with trade

But integrated seamlessly into CK3's feudal grand strategy framework.

### Player Agency

Players should be able to:

1. **Start unlanded** - Merchant adventurer beginning
2. **Build wealth** - Through smart trading
3. **Gain power** - Economic influence over realms
4. **Form republic** - Independent merchant state
5. **Dominate economy** - Control global trade
6. **Create dynasty** - Legendary merchant family

### Economic Depth

The economy should feel:

- **Dynamic** - Constant change and adaptation
- **Interconnected** - Wars affect trade, trade affects politics
- **Realistic** - Historical accuracy in goods and routes
- **Engaging** - Player decisions matter significantly
- **Strategic** - Long-term planning rewarded

### Mod Philosophy

Core principles:

1. **Quality over quantity** - Features done well, not rushed
2. **Historical flavor** - Authentic medieval feel
3. **Balance** - Merchant play equal to traditional CK3
4. **Performance** - Smooth even with heavy use
5. **Compatibility** - Works with other mods
6. **Community** - Listen to player feedback
