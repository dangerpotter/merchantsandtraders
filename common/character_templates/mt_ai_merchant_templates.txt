##############################################
# MERCHANTS AND TRADERS - AI MERCHANT CHARACTER TEMPLATES
# Version: 0.1.0
##############################################
#
# This file defines character templates for AI merchant archetypes.
# Each template creates a distinct personality that affects gameplay:
#
# AGGRESSIVE AI MERCHANT:
# - High risk, high reward strategy
# - Expands quickly, takes long routes
# - Competes directly with player
# - May overextend and fail, but can become very wealthy
# - Creates dynamic tension in markets
#
# CAUTIOUS AI MERCHANT:
# - Low risk, defensive strategy
# - Slow growth, focuses on safe nearby routes
# - Hoards gold, rarely competes
# - Reliable but less threatening competitor
# - Provides steady baseline competition
#
# OPPORTUNISTIC AI MERCHANT:
# - Market timing specialist
# - Exploits booms and crashes
# - Flexible, adaptive strategy
# - Most sophisticated AI behavior
# - Creates interesting economic dynamics
#
# BALANCED AI MERCHANT:
# - Mixed strategy, well-rounded
# - Medium risk/reward
# - Predictable but effective
# - Good baseline competitor
# - Fills out merchant ecosystem
#
# GAMEPLAY DESIGN:
# These templates ensure AI merchants feel like real competitors with
# distinct personalities, not just generic NPCs. Players will learn to
# recognize aggressive rivals who steal markets vs cautious merchants
# who stay in their lane. This creates strategic depth - knowing your
# competition matters.
#
##############################################
