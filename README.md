# Merchants and Traders

A comprehensive merchant and trade route system for Crusader Kings 3.

## Overview

Merchants and Traders transforms the economic landscape of CK3 by introducing a deep and engaging trade system. Build trade networks, manage merchant progression, and integrate trade goods into your realm's economy. Watch as your merchants evolve from humble adventurers into powerful trade republics.

## Features

### Trade Goods System
- Multiple trade goods with regional specializations
- Dynamic supply and demand economy
- Trade goods affect county development and prosperity
- Regional trade specializations based on geography and culture

### Merchant Progression
- **Adventurer Merchants**: Starting level, individual traders seeking opportunities
- **Trade Posts**: Established trading locations with regular routes
- **Trade Companies**: Organized merchant guilds with multiple routes
- **Merchant Republics**: Powerful trading states with political influence

### Trade Routes
- Automated trade route system between counties
- Route profitability based on distance, goods, and local modifiers
- Trade route security affected by realm stability and banditry
- Coastal and overland trade routes with different characteristics

### Economic Integration
- County-level economic modifiers from trade
- Trade income for rulers who support merchant activity
- Trade development affects settlement growth
- Merchant character interactions and decisions

### Future Features
- Merchant dynasties and families
- Trade leagues and alliances
- Piracy and banditry systems
- Trade monopolies and embargoes
- Special trade buildings and improvements

## Installation

### Steam Workshop (Coming Soon)
1. Subscribe to the mod on Steam Workshop
2. Launch CK3 and enable "Merchants and Traders" in the launcher
3. Start a new game or load an existing save

### Manual Installation
1. Download the latest release from the releases page
2. Extract the mod folder to your CK3 mod directory:
   - **Windows**: `Documents\Paradox Interactive\Crusader Kings III\mod\`
   - **Linux**: `~/.local/share/Paradox Interactive/Crusader Kings III/mod/`
   - **Mac**: `~/Documents/Paradox Interactive/Crusader Kings III/mod/`
3. Copy `descriptor.mod` to the same `mod` directory
4. Launch CK3 and enable "Merchants and Traders" in the launcher
5. Start a new game or load an existing save

## Compatibility

- **CK3 Version**: 1.12.* (check descriptor.mod for latest supported version)
- **Save Game Compatible**: Working towards save game compatibility, but initial versions may require new games
- **Mod Compatibility**: Should be compatible with most mods that don't heavily modify economic systems
