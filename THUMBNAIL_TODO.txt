THUMBNAIL REQUIRED FOR STEAM WORKSHOP / PARADOX MODS
=====================================================

Before uploading this mod to Steam Workshop or Paradox Mods, you need to create a thumbnail.png file.

Requirements:
- Steam Workshop: 1:1 ratio, recommended 600x600 pixels, PNG format, 1MB max
- Paradox Mods: Minimum 900x500 pixels, PNG or JPG format, 1MB max

Suggestions:
- Use the mod's theme: medieval trade, merchants, caravans, trade routes
- Include the mod name: "Merchants and Traders"
- Use colors that stand out: gold/bronze for wealth, medieval colors
- Keep text readable at small sizes

Tools you can use:
- GIMP (free): https://www.gimp.org/
- Photoshop
- Canva (online, free tier available)
- Any image editor

Place the file as: thumbnail.png in the mod's root directory
